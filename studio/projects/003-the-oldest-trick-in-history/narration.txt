Poets say the King of Yue trained her for three years, dressed her in silk, and sent her to his enemy's palace. Wu partied. Wu declined. Yue won the war. Stratagem thirty one of thirty six: the beauty trap. The oldest short cut in history. This is the story of the week it came back.

Cut to twenty twenty six. NVIDIA is the most important chip company on earth — the five trillion dollar furnace the entire AI boom is welded to. Jensen Huang co founded it in nineteen ninety three and has run it ever since. And two weeks ago he sent his staff a letter that opens like a tabloid headline and closes like a deposition: I was unfaithful to my wife, and the board has opened an independent investigation into me. Yes. Out loud.

The woman at the center is Alice Huang. No relation. Reporting places her as a top executive at Megaspeed International — a Singapore based data center operator. There is no public photograph of her that anyone can legally license, print, or screen grab. Which, in twenty twenty six, is its own genre of suspicious.

Here is where it stops being awkward and becomes national security. According to reporting based on US intelligence assessments, surveillance unmasked the contact — and identified Alice Huang as a suspected asset of China's Ministry of State Security. A spring twenty twenty six intelligence memo reportedly describes an attempted romance. Of course it does — that is the stratagem. The State Department under Rubio oversaw the memo. Officials were briefed almost a year earlier. Alleged. Not charged. Not proven. But briefed.

Now the receipts get contested. Singapore's government says no such investigation exists. China's foreign ministry called the reports groundless speculation, smears, slander, an attempt to poison business ties. NBC News notes that none of it is independently confirmed, and the CIA said nothing at all. And NVIDIA's board — read this one twice — says it has no reason to believe company secrets were compromised. Which is either the reassurance, or the reason the investigation exists.

So who is Megaspeed? Once known as seven Road. Singapore registered. Data centers across Asia. And a buying arm — Speed Matrix — in Malaysia. Bloomberg traced at least four point eight billion dollars of NVIDIA chip flows through those companies between February twenty twenty four and February twenty twenty six. Jensen Huang called it normal and not unusual. NVIDIA called it thoroughly investigated and debunked last year. Pick whichever sentence you find more comforting.

Why does Washington even care who dates whom? Because in October twenty twenty two, export controls turned advanced AI chips into weapons grade hardware. Suddenly, a CEO's personal life is a customs checkpoint.

So when an alleged asset is reported beside the CEO, markets do the math in one trading day. November ninth: NVIDIA dropped four point three percent — roughly one hundred ninety billion dollars in value, deleted before lunch. A shareholder lawsuit followed, claiming investors were kept in the dark about the disclosures. Allegations, not findings. But the number is real.

So. What do we actually know? Confirmed: he admitted the affair. The board opened an independent investigation. Reported: the M S S link, the memo, the briefing. Denied: Beijing. Singapore. Unknown: whether anything was compromised, whether this was an operation at all, or whether it was simply a terrible coincidence.
