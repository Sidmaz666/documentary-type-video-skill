LOGLINE
A five-minute illustrated essay about a 2025–2026 reporting thread — Nvidia, an AI
compute company in Singapore, and an alleged romance at the centre of an
export-control story — told with dry, modern sarcasm, then traced 2,500 years back
to the oldest spy trick in the book: the beauty trap.

SUMMARY — WHAT THE FILM REPORTS
· A cold open on the ancient stratagem: the King of Wu and Xi Shi, the "beauty trap"
  as later tradition numbers it in the Thirty-Six Stratagems — labelled honestly as
  traditional narrative, not verified history.
· The 2025–2026 thread: Nvidia's founder tells employees he was unfaithful, and the
  board opens an independent investigation after reporting on a foreign intelligence
  effort; the company says it has no reason to believe national security or Nvidia
  secrets were compromised.
· The reported allegation: US intelligence concluded a Nvidia executive identified in
  press accounts as Alice Huang was an asset of China's Ministry of State Security —
  reported by multiple outlets, not publicly confirmed; identities remain as reported.
· The responses: the White House and CIA have not publicly confirmed the memo;
  Singapore's Ministry of Home Affairs says no such investigations were conducted in
  respect of Alice Huang; Beijing calls the reporting groundless speculation.
· The business backdrop: Nvidia's four- and five-trillion-dollar milestones, export
  controls, the H20 licence and charge, the approved H200 arrangement with a 25% cut,
  and a share drop of about 4.3% — roughly $190 billion — when the story broke, plus a
  securities class action that alleges disclosure claims only, not espionage.
· The playbook: a quick, honest history of the trick — Mata Hari in 1917 (and the 1999
  declassified files that found nothing), East Germany's "Romeos", Monica Witt, and
  Christine Fang — then the open question the film leaves standing: an operation, or
  human weakness? Public evidence does not settle it.

WHAT IS ALLEGED VS ESTABLISHED
The film separates the record as follows. Official documents and statements — Nvidia's
newsroom letter and board statement, export-control rules, the Singapore MHA statement,
the FBI's 2019 indictment — are treated as established. The intelligence conclusion and
the reported romance are treated as reported allegations: attributed, hedged, and never
stated as proven. No legal conclusions are drawn; the securities case is described as
disclosure claims, not an espionage finding. Names of non-public individuals are used
as reported, with an on-screen guard line where an image could be mistaken for evidence.

HOW IT IS MADE
Picture is a code-drawn paper world (Bun + @napi-rs/canvas): one continuous camera move
over a hand-inked board, with licensed real photographs and archival prints composited as
physical props — grade-matched, credited off-frame. No AI-generated imagery or video is
used anywhere. Sound is the narration plus synthesized SFX, with a licensed public-domain
classical recording as the constant music bed, sidechain-ducked under speech (MUSIC LAW).
No license, credit or source text appears on any frame; attribution lives in this file,
assets/assets-credits.csv, and the video description (house LAW 16).
